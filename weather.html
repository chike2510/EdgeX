How the edge is calculated
1
Pull ensemble forecasts from 4 weather supercomputers — GFS, ECMWF, GEM, ICON — each running dozens of simulations.
2
Convert to probabilities — if 110 of 169 runs show rain >10mm, that's 65% implied probability.
3
Compare to Bayse market odds — enter the market price below. If market says 80% but model says 55%, that 25% gap is your edge.
4
Edge classification: >15% gap = HIGH edge · 8–15% = MEDIUM · <8% = LOW.
