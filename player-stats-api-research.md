# Free football player-statistics API research

## API-Football

Official site: https://www.api-football.com/

The site states that the free plan is always free, does not require a credit card, and provides 100 requests per day. It lists players, lineups, statistics, events, fixtures, and head-to-head among the available endpoint families. It also states that real-time matches/events are updated every 15 seconds and that several years of data are available, while noting that free-plan season availability is limited versus paid plans.

## football-data.org

Official documentation: https://www.football-data.org/documentation/api

The documentation exposes Competition, Match, Team, Standing, and Player resources, and describes squad/lineup/event information. The registered free plan is described as 10 requests per minute; unauthenticated access is much more restricted. The service is useful for fixtures, competitions, teams, squads, and basic player/person resources, but is generally less suitable than API-Football for rich per-match player event/statistics or high-frequency live player props.

## Sportmonks

Official free plan: https://www.sportmonks.com/football-api/free-plan/

Sportmonks advertises a no-expiration free football plan with live data/events, fixtures, teams/player data, and advanced statistics, but its free-plan page limits coverage to the Danish Superliga and Scottish Premiership including play-offs. The page does not publish a general request quota in the visible plan description. Its player-statistics documentation: https://docs.sportmonks.com/v3/tutorials-and-guides/tutorials/statistics/players-statistics, describes season player statistics and fixture-level statistics, including shots, passes, crosses, tackles, interceptions, clearances, duels, dribbles, fouls, big chances, minutes, rating, and xG. It explicitly excludes physical/tracking metrics such as distance covered, sprints, and top speed.
